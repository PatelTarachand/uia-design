UIA Hosts Industrial Excellence Summit 2024

Urla Industries Association successfully organized the Industrial Excellence Summit 2024, bringing together industry leaders, government officials, and stakeholders to discuss sustainable industrial development and innovation strategies for Chhattisgarh.

Summit Highlights

The Industrial Excellence Summit 2024, held at the prestigious Convention Center in Raipur, marked a significant milestone in UIA's commitment to fostering industrial growth and innovation in Chhattisgarh. The event witnessed unprecedented participation from over 300 industry leaders, government officials, and key stakeholders from across the state.

The summit focused on three core themes: Sustainable Industrial Development, Digital Transformation in Manufacturing, and Policy Framework for Industrial Growth. These themes were carefully selected to address the current challenges and future opportunities facing the industrial sector in our region.

Distinguished Speakers

Hon. Chief Minister

State of Chhattisgarh

Delivered the keynote address on "Vision 2030: Industrial Transformation of Chhattisgarh"

Dr. Rajesh Kumar

Secretary, Ministry of Heavy Industries

Spoke on "National Industrial Policy and State-Level Implementation"

The summit also featured panel discussions with CEOs from major UIA member companies including Steel Authority of India, NTPC, and other leading industrial houses. These sessions provided valuable insights into best practices, challenges, and innovative solutions being implemented across different sectors.

Key Outcomes & Resolutions

Green Industrial Corridor Initiative

Launch of a comprehensive green industrial corridor project to promote sustainable manufacturing practices across the Urla Industrial Area.

Digital Infrastructure Enhancement

Commitment to upgrade digital infrastructure and promote Industry 4.0 adoption among member companies with government support.

Skill Development Program

Establishment of a state-of-the-art skill development center to train workforce in emerging technologies and advanced manufacturing techniques.

Strategic Partnerships

One of the major highlights of the summit was the signing of multiple Memorandums of Understanding (MOUs) between UIA and various stakeholders:

  • UIA - State Government Partnership: For streamlined industrial approvals and policy implementation
  • Technology Collaboration: With leading technology institutes for research and development initiatives
  • Financial Support Framework: With banking partners for easier access to industrial financing

Leadership Statement

"The Industrial Excellence Summit 2024 represents a pivotal moment in our journey towards making Chhattisgarh a leading industrial destination in India. The collaborative spirit demonstrated by all stakeholders gives us confidence that we are on the right path to achieving sustainable and inclusive industrial growth."

UIA President Urla Industries Association

Looking Ahead

The success of the Industrial Excellence Summit 2024 has set the stage for a series of follow-up initiatives. UIA is committed to implementing the resolutions and recommendations that emerged from the summit. A dedicated task force has been constituted to monitor the progress of various initiatives and ensure timely implementation.

The next phase will focus on creating detailed action plans for each initiative, establishing timelines, and setting up monitoring mechanisms. UIA will continue to serve as the bridge between industry and government, facilitating smooth implementation of policies and programs that benefit the entire industrial ecosystem.
