Our Foundation

Urla Industries Association (UIA) is a nonprofit membership organization for a wide range of industries located in Urla Industrial Area, represented by their Directors/Partner/Senior Executives. The association was founded in 1990 with a vision to create a unified voice for the industrial community.

Over the past three decades, UIA has evolved into Chhattisgarh's most influential industry association, representing diverse sectors from steel and power to chemicals and manufacturing, driving sustainable industrial growth.

Structure & Expertise

UIA's robust structure and diverse expertise enable us to be highly responsive to our members' evolving needs. We actively assess and shape legislative and regulatory activities in close collaboration with executives from member companies, ensuring that industry voices are heard at the highest levels.

Deep Understanding

We maintain an in-depth understanding of our members' business operations, challenges, and opportunities. This comprehensive knowledge is essential for effectively translating their technology, business, and policy requirements into actionable strategies and government advocacy.

Our Mission & Impact

UIA serves as the primary representative for Small, Medium, and Large industries in the Urla Industrial Area. Our core mission is to bridge the gap between industries and government, facilitating smoother communication and collaboration for mutual benefit.

Over the years, UIA has been instrumental in driving basic infrastructure developments, influencing industrial policies, and creating an enabling environment that has significantly contributed to industrial growth in our region.

Our Vision

To be India's leading Industry association, creating a better world for our members, society, and the nation through sustainable industrial development and innovation.

Our Core Focus

UIA delivers exceptional value through:

  • Strategic government liaison and advocacy
  • Centre of excellence and innovation hub
  • Investment facilitation and partnerships
  • Sustainable industrial development
