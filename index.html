About Us

About Urla Industries Association

Urla Industries Association (UIA) is a nonprofit membership organization for a wide range of industries located in Urla Industrial Area, represented by their Directors/Partner/Senior Executives, the association was founded in 1990.

Our Structure

UIA's structure and wide range of expertise allow the association to be highly responsive to our members' needs, assessing and shaping legislative and regulatory activity in collaboration with executives from member companies.

Our Understanding

UIA strives for a detailed understanding of the business operations for our members. Such understanding is vital in translating their technology, business, and policy needs into action.

Our Mission

UIA has been working as representative of Small, Medium and Large industries of Urla Industrial Area. To bridge the gap between industries and government has been the main objective of UIA. In past years, UIA has worked on basic infrastructure developments, industrial policies etc. that helped Industries to grow.

Our Vision

India's leading Industry association creating a better world for its members, society and country.

Our Objectives

UIA exists to deliver value to its members by:

  • Acting as a bridge between members and government
  • Building centre of excellence tool room
  • Being a point of contact for investments

Our Objectives

UIA exists to deliver value to its members by:

Government Bridge

Acting as a bridge between its members and government and other legal bodies, in order to make the relationship smoother and better.

Centre of Excellence

Building a centre of excellence tool room for its members for better technology, precise results, more scientific data etc.

Investment Hub

Being a point of contact for anyone seeking investment, joint-partnership or merger etc. in Urla Industrial Area.

Marketing Events

Conducting marketing events for the promotion of the member industries.

Recognition of Excellence

Recognition of excellence through Awards programmes to celebrate and motivate outstanding achievements in the industrial sector.
